Student Task Tracker

A simple and professional academic task management system designed to help students organize their assignments, deadlines and daily academic activities.

✨ Key Features

Everything included in the Student Task Tracker.

📝

Task Management

Add, edit, complete and delete academic tasks from one organized workspace.

⏰

Deadline Tracking

Easily identify overdue, today's, tomorrow's and upcoming assignments.

🚦

Priority Management

Mark tasks as High, Medium or Low priority to organize your workload effectively.

📊

Productivity Analytics

Monitor completed tasks, pending tasks, completion rate and subject workload.

🔍

Search & Filters

Quickly find tasks using search, status, priority and sorting options.

💾

SQLite Database

Task information is stored locally using a lightweight SQLite database.

Project Purpose

The Student Task Tracker is designed to help students maintain better academic organization. Instead of managing assignments using notebooks or scattered applications, students can maintain their tasks, deadlines and priorities in one centralized system.
